After posting to [peterme.com](http://www.peterme.com/) about how I’d like to see [more tools available for the IA community](http://peterme.com/archives/00000330.html#5), I got an email from Lisa Chan from [Stanford](http://gsb.stanford.edu/) saying that she was also looking into creating a Search Log Analyser, and that if I was going to be working on one, why don’t we work together! This is really cool, I love how the Internet allows things like this to happen. I am here in Perth, WA, knowing all of 2 other IAs personally, and yet I will now be collaborating with another IA from one of the largest Universities in the US to build a tool to help all IAs.

You rock Internet.
