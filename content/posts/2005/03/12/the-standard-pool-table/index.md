This is the pool table at The Standard, Downtown LA. It’s pretty cool-looking, nice and big, but it has a couple of large drink stains on it, and the rails are busted up so balls jump off them. Oh, and it’s right next to a massive glass window – so be careful with that (sorry Mr Security).
