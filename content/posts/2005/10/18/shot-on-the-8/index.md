# [Shot on the 8](http://dentedreality.com.au/2005/10/18/shot-on-the-8/)

Here’s Ray, lining up for the winning shot in a drunken game at Spinners, Waikiki, HI. He makes this shot, using a lot of English, shooting just in front of the dot, and then bringing it backwards to make the 8 without following it in. This is the first frame of the video I happened to take of him shooting.
