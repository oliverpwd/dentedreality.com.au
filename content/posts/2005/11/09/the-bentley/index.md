# [“The” Bentley](http://dentedreality.com.au/2005/11/09/the-bentley/)

This is Peter’s favourite Bentley. At this point, it’s almost completely hand-built, and was fully-restored after it burned to a crisp after catching on fire on a cross-Australian Bentley tour a couple years ago. It’s an amazing looking vehicle, although in this shot it’s being cleaned down and having a few parts fixed up, so it’s not look as crash-hot.
