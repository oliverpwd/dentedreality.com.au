# [That One Guy](http://dentedreality.com.au/2006/01/28/that-one-guy/)

Amazing, crazy show. This guy plays a…. "magic pipe", with strings, sometimes like a guitar, sometimes like an upright bass, sometimes just slapping it with his hands. He also has a kick-drum, and brought in a woodsaw for one track! Check him out at [www.that1guy.com/](http://www.that1guy.com/). Saw him @ Warfield.
