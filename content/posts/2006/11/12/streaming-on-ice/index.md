# [Streaming On Ice](http://dentedreality.com.au/2006/11/12/streaming-on-ice/)

This shot is a stitched together copy of a number of shots, all taken in sequence as one of the skiiers came down the jump.

It was put together by layering each progressive shot over the previous one, then lining them up (using 40% opacity), then clipping out everything except for the actual skier. I think it turned out quite nicely ![:)](http://i0.wp.com/dentedreality.com.au/wp-includes/images/smilies/icon_smile.gif?w=607)
