[GeoPress – GeoRSS – Trac](http://www.georss.org/trac/trac.cgi/wiki/GeoPress)

> Awesome plugin for WordPress that allows users to quickly and easily embed location information in blog posts. You can then embed a dynamic map, Microformat adr and geo output, and adds GeoRSS to the RSS output.
