[Aggregate plugin for WordPress](http://robm.me.uk/projects/plugins/wordpress/aggregate)

> Aggregate allows you to display the contents of an RSS feed within your posts. It automatically fetches, parses and caches the contents of the RSS feed, and displays the entries—with links—in an HTML unordered list.
