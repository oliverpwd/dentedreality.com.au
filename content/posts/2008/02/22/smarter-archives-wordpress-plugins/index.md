[Smarter Archives « WordPress Plugins](http://wordpress.org/extend/plugins/smarter-archives/)

> This plugin provides you with a template tag for your archives page, which presents navigating the archives in a unique way. It has a row for each year with posts, and every month in that year with posts has a link to the month.
