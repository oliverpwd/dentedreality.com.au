# [Flower + Parasite](http://dentedreality.com.au/2010/01/10/flower-parasite/)

The yellow stringy stuff in this picture is actually a parasite which grows all over the other ground-cover plants and strangles them. It looks almost like silly string or spider webs and is sticky — very strange!
