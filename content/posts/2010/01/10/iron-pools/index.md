# [Iron Pools](http://dentedreality.com.au/2010/01/10/iron-pools/)

This is a small, natural spring, where the water is very heavy in iron, so it colors everything around it red. The grass at the edge of the pool was "rusty" with iron. Rinsing your hands in here made them feel strangely clean (and tingly because the water was so cold!)
