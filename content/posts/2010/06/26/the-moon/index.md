# [The Moon](http://dentedreality.com.au/2010/06/26/the-moon/)

At around 11pm, the (very full, very bright) moon came over the crest of the Columns of the Giants. It was a truly impressive sight. It was so bright that I could clearly see everything around me, and didn’t bother using a headlamp or anything while getting around.
