![The Year Without Pants](http://dentedreality.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/1118660633.jpg)

Today is kind of exciting, although it’s been a long time coming so it’s not much of a surprise for me ![:)](http://i0.wp.com/dentedreality.com.au/wp-includes/images/smilies/icon_smile.gif?w=607) Today [Scott Berkun](http://scottberkun.com), the author of books such as [*The Myths of Innovation*](http://www.amazon.com/dp/1449389627/tag=scottberkunco-20), and [*Confessions of a Public Speaker*](http://www.amazon.com/Confessions-Public-Speaker-English/dp/1449301959/tag=scottberkunco-20), releases his latest book, *[The Year Without Pants: WordPress.com and the Future of Work](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/1118660633/scottberkunco-20/).*

The book gives an inside look at what it’s like to work at [Automattic](http://automattic.com), and to work on something like [WordPress.com](http://wordpress.com). Scott was my direct team lead (of the team that I now lead) while he was at Automattic, so the book contains a lot of personal interaction with yours truly. It also happens to be a fun read with a bunch of interesting insights into distributed teams, management, and the open-source-based culture we have at Automattic, and which may well be the future of many more companies.

I’ve read versions all the way back to some of the first drafts, and am right now reading the “final” version which I received in hard copy. You should [go get it](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/1118660633/scottberkunco-20/) and read it as well.
