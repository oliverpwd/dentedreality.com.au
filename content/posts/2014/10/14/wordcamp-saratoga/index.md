Through some lucky scheduling, I was able to attend both [LevelUp Con](http://dentedreality.com.au/2014/10/14/levelup-con/ "LevelUp Con") and [WordCamp Saratoga](http://2014.saratoga.wordcamp.org/) in a single trip. I spoke at WordCamp about how to build a quick [Backbone.js](http://backbonejs.org/) application which used [WordPress](http://wordpress.org) as the backend (interfacing via [the REST API](http://wp-api.org/)). I thought my talk went OK, although I didn’t love it to be honest, and in hindsight I kind of wish I’d dived a bit harder into some better examples of how Backbone works with Views and whatnot. Here are the slides I used:

 
  **[WordPress as the Backbone(.js)](https://www.slideshare.net/beaulebens/wordpress-as-the-backbonejs "WordPress as the Backbone(.js)")**  from **[Beau Lebens](http://www.slideshare.net/beaulebens)**
