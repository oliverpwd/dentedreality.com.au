Yesterday I went up to Breck with [Erika](https://teachershavelivestoo.com/). She went snowboarding, and I tried out my new [MSR snowshoes](https://www.rei.com/product/875159/msr-evo-snowshoes). I started out at the [Breck Nordic Center](http://breckenridgenordic.com/), and hiked for maybe 3 miles or so, before heading back to Peak 8 to meet up for bloody marys and fries. It was a gorgeous day, and snowshoeing was fun. I ran into a few people who said there were moose around, but I didn’t see them unfortunately. Being out there made me really curious about what it’d be like to do with a full pack on, and be going backpacking in full winter. That’s still on my list of things to try out. Here are some pics from the trails I took (Willow, Engleman, Peaks Trail).
