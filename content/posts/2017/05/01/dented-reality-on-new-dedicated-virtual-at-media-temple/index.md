I’ve completed the migration of [dentedreality.com.au](http://dentedreality.com.au/) (along with all my other domains, excluding this one) to a new dedicated virtual server with [Media Temple](https://mediatemple.net/). Mad props to them, they’ve been great in helping me handle some pieces of the process, which has gone relatively smoothly. Things I’m looking forward to on the new host include:

* Working with Plesk Onyx
* Trying out Let’s Encrypt 🔐
* PHP 7 😍
* Hopefully consolidating and simplifying some things

This site ([beau.blog](https://beau.blog/)) is now my main blogging/writing space, with Dented Reality being more of a full archive of my digital footsteps.
