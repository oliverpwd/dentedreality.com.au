In [`7072886`](https://github.com/beaulebens/keyring/commit/7072886bdf4b2dd8e43871688c2cde1ff9340049) I added a first swing at a [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) Service definition to Keyring. It’s based heavily on the Google Analytics one that was [recently submitted via PR](https://github.com/beaulebens/keyring/pull/22). It’s not part of an official release yet. I’m curious about enabling people to import the videos they publish on YouTube into a WordPress install though, as that feels like something that folks who use it heavily would want as part of their web presence.

![YouTube Service for Keyring](https://i1.wp.com/dentedreality.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/youtube-keyring.png?fit=1100%2C226&ssl=1)![YouTube Service for Keyring](https://i1.wp.com/dentedreality.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/youtube-keyring.png?fit=1100%2C226&ssl=1)

Next step will be adding an importer for YouTube, which I'll probably aim to do over the coming weeks.
