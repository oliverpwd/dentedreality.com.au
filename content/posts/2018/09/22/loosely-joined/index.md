I had to chuckle recently when I realized just how complex some of the systems in my life are, even if I rarely think about the details.

1. Most nights, I weigh myself on a [Withings wifi scale](https://www.withings.com/ca/en/body-plus) (an older version).
2. That data syncs over [Google Wifi](https://store.google.com/us/product/google_wifi), via a [Comcast Xfinity connection](https://www.xfinity.com/), to the [Nokia/Withings Health Mate](https://www.withings.com/nl/en/health-mate) service.
3. Then [MyFitnessPal](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/) automatically syncs the data from Health Mate, and keeps a copy,
4. before [Garmin Connect](https://connect.garmin.com/) also syncs a copy to integrate back with other data (like heart rate and activities) .
5. If I’ve been doing specific activities, then some of the data from Garmin Connect (although not that weight info) will also sync out to [Strava](https://www.strava.com/) and create activity records.

The last step remaining is that I want to hook up [my copy of WordPress](http://dentedreality.com.au) to [sync all of the data back](https://wordpress.org/plugins/keyring-social-importers/#description) so that I have a copy under my direct control.
