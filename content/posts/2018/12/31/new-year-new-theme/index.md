To ring in 2019, I’m changing this blog’s theme to Twenty Nineteen, the new default WordPress theme, designed and primarily created by my excellent colleague, [Allan Cole](https://www.allancole.com/) (check out his music, published as [The Stuyvesants](https://www.thestuyvesants.com/), they’re groovy).

Apart from being pretty similar to, but a nice upgrade from [the previous theme here](https://wordpress.com/theme/independent-publisher-2), Twenty Nineteen also harnesses the full power of [Gutenberg](https://wordpress.org/gutenberg/), the new WordPress Block Editor. I’m going to convert some posts to blocks so that I can use some of the better gallery options and whatnot, and will be using Gutenberg for everything going forward. It also reminds me a bit of the styling used throughout [Instapaper](https://www.instapaper.com/), which I’ve spent a lot of time in lately 🙂

Happy New Year!
